📘 Trevora — Pan-India Tourist Safety & Travel Intelligence System

Comprehensive Technical & Feature Report | Architecture, Machine Learning, Real-Time Ingestion & Blockchain
Project Overview: An intelligent, real-time safety monitoring and emergency assistance application built for tourists across all of India. Combines a React Native / Expo Mobile Client with an asynchronous FastAPI Backend running LightGBM Gradient Boosted Regression, SHAP Explainability, OpenStreetMap infrastructure mapping, live Open-Meteo weather hazard checks, automated news ingestion, and Decentralized Web3 / Blockchain Digital Identity.

1. System Architecture & Information Flow

The system operates across three interconnected layers:

  1. Mobile Client Layer: React Native with Expo Router, Leaflet/Mapbox vector cartography, turn-by-turn safe routing, and emergency SOS dispatch.
  2. Backend Processing & Scoring Layer: FastAPI framework with continuous spatial indexing (Uber H3 Resolution-8 hexagons, ~460m radius).
  3. Dynamic Data & Verification Layer:
    • OpenStreetMap Overpass API: Automated scanning of police stations, emergency hospitals, and municipal lighting within a 15 km radius.
    • Open-Meteo Weather API: Live hourly rain precipitation, wind speed, and convective storm detection.
    • GDELT & Google News RSS: Real-time automated scraping and NLP classification of civil unrest, natural hazards, and crimes.
    • Blockchain Identity Layer: Cryptographic verification of traveler identities, permits, and emergency health profiles.

2. Core Artificial Intelligence & Safety Features

2.1. Real-Time Dynamic Safety Score (0 – 100)

Calculates an objective, continuous risk score for any location, neighborhood, or tourist spot in India based on:

  • SAFE (80 – 100) Dense police posts, rapid medical emergency access, high public illumination, active surveillance corridors.
  • CAUTION (60 – 79) High tidal warnings, limited late-night lighting, or isolated non-violent incidents.
  • AVOID (< 60) Active storm surge advisories, extreme flooding, landslides, or active civil curfews.

2.2. Explainable AI with SHAP ("Why this score?")

Rather than providing an uninterpretable score, the model uses SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) to explain the factors contributing to the rating:

  • "Tourist Police Assistance Booth within 250m (+14 pts)"
  • "General Emergency Hospital reachable within 1.4 km (+8 pts)"
  • "High CCTV coverage along heritage street (+6 pts)"
  • "High coastal swell advisory (-8 pts)"

2.3. Dynamic Pan-India City Registry & On-Demand OSM Scanner

Pre-configured with 70+ Indian cities and major tourist destinations (Delhi, Mumbai, Agra, Jaipur, Varanasi, Goa, Hampi, Kochi, Munnar, Darjeeling, etc.). When a tourist searches any new Indian town, town square, or landmark:

  1. Resolves GPS coordinates via Nominatim Geocoder (countrycodes=in).
  2. Scans OpenStreetMap Overpass for police stations (amenity=police) and hospitals (amenity=hospital).
  3. Caches feature distances in local SQLite (city_cache.db) for 7 days, guaranteeing sub-second response times.

2.4. Automated 20-Minute News Ingestion & Recency Decay

A background worker (APScheduler) periodically ingests news from the GDELT Project and Google News RSS across Indian states:

  • NLP Incident Classification: Classifies headlines into 12 categories: theft, harassment, weather, traffic, scam, accident, protest, curfew, etc.
  • Jaccard Deduplication: Eliminates duplicate articles from multiple publishers.
  • 36-Hour Exponential Decay: Recent incidents apply maximum score deductions; penalties decrease by 50% after 36 hours and phase out entirely after 72 hours.

2.5. Live Weather Hazard Integration

Connects to Open-Meteo's weather models covering India. Extreme rainfall (>10 mm/h), high wind gusts, or thunderstorms immediately deduct points and trigger hazard warnings.

2.6. Crowdsourced Incident Reporting

Tourists can submit real-time reports of hazards (scams, broken infrastructure, harassment, waterlogging) directly through the app, immediately updating the neighborhood's risk level.

3. Blockchain Login & Decentralized Digital Tourist ID

To eliminate centralized database security risks, identity theft, and credential vulnerabilities, the application integrates a Decentralized Identity (DID) & Web3 Authentication module:

Capability Technical Architecture Traveler Safety Benefit
Blockchain Digital Tourist ID Issues a cryptographically signed Decentralized Identifier (did:tourist:...) tied to a Web3 wallet. Non-forgeable, tamper-proof digital tourist permit recognized at park gates, checkpoints, and police desks.
Tamper-Proof Emergency Medical Records Stores cryptographic hashes of critical medical info (blood group, allergies, pre-existing conditions) on-chain. Guarantees search-and-rescue teams receive unalterable medical facts during emergencies.
Cryptographic SOS Verification Signs distress dispatches with the traveler's asymmetric private key. Prevents spoofed or malicious SOS alerts, ensuring emergency teams deploy with maximum priority.
Web3 Wallet Single Sign-On (SSO) Passwordless authentication using asymmetric signature verification (MetaMask / WalletConnect / Web3Auth). Eliminates password leaks, database breaches, and credential stuffing vulnerabilities.

4. Mobile Client Features (React Native / Expo)

  • India Safety Radar Home Banner: Shows real-time zone safety ratings, safe status tags, and quick-action navigation.
  • Live Safety Dashboard Screen: Quick preset chips for major Indian monuments (Taj Mahal, India Gate, Gateway of India, Jaipur, Hampi, Fort Kochi) plus free-text search for any location across India.
  • Interactive Navigation & Safe Points: Live GPS breadcrumb tracking with emergency quick-filters for nearby Hospitals, Police Stations, and Pharmacies. Includes a "Save Safe Point" feature for returning to hotels or safe havens.
  • One-Tap Emergency SOS: Sends emergency SMS alerts containing the user's exact coordinates to trusted emergency contacts.
  • Top Rated Safe Tourist Destinations: Curated nationwide rankings of attractions, complete with verified security badges, optimal visiting hours, and descriptions.

6. REST API Endpoints

  • GET /safety-score?place={name}&lat={lat}&lon={lon} — Live safety evaluation for any place in India.
  • GET /places/safest?place={name}&radius_km=15 — Safest attractions nearby.
  • POST /trip — Generates a safe, custom itinerary sorted by safety or interest.
  • GET /incidents?hours=48 — Active live incidents feed.
  • POST /incidents/report — Submits a user-reported incident.
  • GET /cities — Returns all 70+ pre-cached Indian cities and coordinates.
  • POST /api/chat — SafeTrip Buddy conversational AI with location context.
  • GET /api/history/{client_id}/{chat_id} — Chat conversation persistence.
  • GET /health — API health and status check.
